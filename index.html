Welcome to your Anime & Manga Archive!

This is your personal space to track everything you watch and read across the years.

Getting Started

  1. Add titles: Click "+ Add title" in the top right. You can add a single series or paste a whole list at once.
  2. Fetch metadata: Open a title and click "Fetch Metadata" to auto-pull the poster, banner, studio, description, cast, creators and episode/chapter list from AniList.
  3. Track progress: Tick episodes as you watch them, jot notes on individual episodes, mark titles as watching / completed / dropped and give them a personal score.
  4. Organize: Use collections for fun themes and franchises to group related titles (Fate, Gundam...).

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • ← → Navigate between titles in a list
  • Esc Close modals
  • Enter Perform search
